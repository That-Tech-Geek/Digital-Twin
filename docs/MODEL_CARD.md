# Model card
Task: personalized glucose forecasting and impending hypoglycemia risk estimation.
Outputs: probabilistic glucose trajectories and threshold-crossing probabilities at 30/60/120 minutes.
Population: insulin-treated diabetes cohorts, primarily T1D in open datasets. Indian T2D is future deployment/validation work.
Limitations: small patient counts, domain shift, CGM noise/lag, missing modalities, simplified IOB proxy, counterfactual non-identifiability and calibration drift.
